Collaborative research and poster in Finland

Assisted with research within the Opening Pandora’s Box project at the University of Helsinki, investigating temperature-dependent VOC release from Arctic permafrost and the SOA formation capacity of these emissions after oxidation. The study combines soil incubation, Vocus PTR-MS, PAM chamber and NO3-CIMS.

D. L. Pereira, M. Passananti, D. Zhang, H. Timonen, M. Väliranta, F. Bianchi. Assessing the impact of permafrost thaw emissions from Arctic regions. [Collaborative poster]
